Echo's Binary Race

  2. The three slots are worth 4, 2 and 1. Fill them left to right.
  3. Catch a 1 to use a slot's value. Catch a 0 to skip it.

Example5 = 4 + 0 + 1, so catch 1 0 1

Tap a lane or press the ← → keys to move Echo. Mistakes are OK: the race never ends early and every gate opens.
